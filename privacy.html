HDR Story — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

HDR Story is an iOS app that turns your HDR photos into short videos for Instagram Stories. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos stay on your device.

Photos and videos

Photos you pick are processed entirely on your device. The app has no server that receives your photos or the videos made from them. Videos are saved to your Photos library only if you allow it, using the “add to Photos” permission; the app does not read the rest of your library.

Sharing to Instagram

When you tap the share button, the app hands the finished video to the Instagram app on your device through the system clipboard and opens Instagram. The clipboard entry expires after five minutes. HDR Story does not sign in to your Instagram or Facebook account, does not use the Instagram API, and receives no data from Meta. What happens to the video inside Instagram is covered by Instagram’s own privacy policy.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a technical log on your device so problems can be investigated; it leaves the device only if you export and send it yourself. Anonymous usage statistics are off by default and are sent only if you turn them on in Settings. They contain no photos, file names, location, contacts, or account identifiers, and you can turn them off at any time.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Videos saved to Photos stay in your library until you delete them there. Since no personal data is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete; if you have questions, write to the address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
